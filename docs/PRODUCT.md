# Shortlist: product

## In one line
Shortlist helps anyone get a job faster. It makes a CV for every job that looks great and says nothing untrue, tracks every application, and gets you ready for the interview.

## Who it's for
Anyone applying for jobs in English, anywhere:
- Students and people looking for their first job.
- Workers moving up or moving on.
- People changing careers.
- People applying abroad, for example from Nepal and South Asia to the Gulf, Europe or remote jobs.

They aren't writers or designers. Most of them apply on their phone at night.

## The problems we solve

## Features

### 1. Your profile (the only source of facts)
- Three ways in: upload a PDF or DOCX, paste the text, or **build one with help**. For an upload or paste, AI or the basic reader turns it into editable sections.
- **Build one with help** (`/app/profile/build`) is for people with no CV yet. It has six short steps (about you, work, studies, skills and languages, summary, a look), with a live preview:
  - Under each job or project, people write roughly what they did. **Turn into bullet points** proposes up to five bullets from those notes only. Any word the fact check can't find in the notes or profile is named, and that bullet starts unticked.
  - "No work yet?" switches to projects, volunteering and part-time work.
  - Skills come as one-tap chips, starting with ones their own words mention.
  - **Write it for me** drafts the summary from what's entered, fact-checked.
  - The last step shows four recommended templates using their own CV. Finish sets the default look and opens Studio.
  - Every step saves, so leaving halfway loses nothing, and coming back opens the first step that's still empty.
  - Without an AI key, plain-code versions run instead: they tidy the person's own sentences and assemble the summary from the profile, adding nothing.
- Edit everything: contact details, summary, experience, education, skills, certifications, languages, projects and an optional photo.
- Download it as JSON at any time from Settings.

### 2. Jobs and the match check
- Add a job by link or by pasting the text. If a site blocks reading (LinkedIn often does), Shortlist asks you to paste it.
- See title, company, location and salary, with the must-haves and nice-to-haves separated.
- The match check groups requirements into three kinds:
  - **shown in your work**, with the line that proves it
  - **only in your skills list**
  - **missing**
- Keywords are highlighted in the post, and the score is labelled as a guide.

### 3. Tailored CV
- AI suggests a new summary, rewritten bullets and a better order for bullets and skills.
- Each change shows a before-and-after diff, and you accept or reject it.
- The no-invention check blocks changes that add facts. Blocked changes can't be accepted.
- Pick a template for each job. Download as PDF (free) or Word (with a pass).

### 4. CV Studio
- **Gallery** of 100 templates in 12 categories, with filters, search and "Recommended for you".
- **Live preview with your own CV**, not lorem ipsum.
- **Editor** to make your own template:
  - layout, fonts, colours, headings, bullets and density
  - photo, page size, and section order and visibility
  - live A4 or Letter preview with page breaks, fit to one or two pages, undo and redo
- **Checks:** ATS safety, contrast, page count, empty sections and photo advice for your target country.
- **"What an ATS reads":** the plain text a recruiter's system gets from your CV.
- **CV strength meter:** quantified bullets, action verbs, bullet length, missing sections and dates. Each point links to the field to fix.
- **My templates:** save up to 50, duplicate, rename and set a default. They're private.

### 5. Cover letter
Written for the job from your real experience, then checked for invented facts. It's editable and uses the same look as your CV.

### 6. Interview prep
- Questions from the job post and your background, including honest gap questions.
- Type an answer and get a score, what works, what to improve and a suggested rewrite.

### 7. Tracker and follow-ups
- Stages: Saved, Applied, Interviewing, Offer and Closed. Switch between the board and a table, and drag cards between stages.
- Dates, notes and the interview date.
- 7 days after applying with no answer, Today and a daily email remind you, with a follow-up message ready to copy.

### 8. Today and Progress
- **Today:**
  - the next best action, KPIs with 8-week trends, follow-ups due and a mini pipeline
  - the strength meter, upcoming interviews and a weekly goal ring
- **Progress:** applications, replies, interviews and response rate over time.

### 9. Account, plans and payments
- Sign up, confirm your email, reset your password, export your data and delete your account.
- Free plan, or a Job-hunt pass bought once (no auto-renew) through Paddle in USD or Khalti in NPR.

## Principles
1. **Nothing made up.** Every fact comes from the user's profile. Code checks it, so we don't rely on the model behaving.
2. **Honest labels.** Scores are guides, ATS warnings are real, and gaps are never hidden or filled in.
3. **Works without AI.** Every AI feature has a plain-code fallback.
4. **Never hold users' money.** Paddle and Khalti take payments.
5. **Plain words.** Short sentences, no jargon and no hype.
6. **Phone first.** Every page works on a 375px screen.

## Not in scope (and why)
- **Auto-applying to jobs:** it breaks job sites' terms and gets accounts banned.
- **A job search engine:** we're not competing with LinkedIn or Indeed. Users bring the jobs.
- **Native mobile apps:** the web app works on phones.
- **Languages other than English:** for now.
- **Sharing templates publicly:** they're private for now, and sharing would need moderation.
- **Skill rating bars on CVs:** they mean nothing to recruiters and confuse an ATS.
